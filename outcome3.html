Search, as the name implies, is the process of AI exploring its search space—consisting of every possible path, solution, and state that it can evaluate—to reach its goal. Search often comes with five main components: initial state (your starting point), goal test (test if you are at your destination), action (describe the possible step you can take during the path), transition model (describe the result after you move), path cost (the total expense of actions you took to reach your goal). There are also some other important terms such as search problem, what you are trying to solve; search tree, every possible path AI explored; search strategy, the behavior of the algorithm; state space, all the possible states of the problem; and environment, how the path and state look. There are also three fundatemematal serach algorithm, which are Breadth-First Search (BFS), Depth-First Search (DFS), and A* Search.
